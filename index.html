Why We're Qruising

Chicago's quantum community, all in one place.

Chicago's quantum scene is growing fast, from university labs to startups to the companies scaling quantum hardware and software. But the people doing that work don't get many chances to just be in a room together. Quantum Qruise is that chance: one relaxed evening to catch up, meet new people, and celebrate how far this community has come.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I get there?

Boarding starts at 5:15 PM, fifteen minutes before we shove off. We cast off right at 5:30, so try not to cut it too close.

Where do we board?

We board the Leading Lady at 112 E. Wacker Dr, on the Chicago Riverwalk. Discounted parking is available across the street at 111 E. Wacker Dr. Look for the Quantum Qruise team near the dock and we'll point you the right way.

What time does the boat get back?

We dock again by 7:30 PM. Plan to be off the boat by 7:45 so we can wrap things up on time.

Who's this event for?

Quantum Qruise brings together, for one relaxed night on the water:

  • Professors, post-docs, and researchers
  • Startup founders
  • Investors
  • Quantum ecosystem leaders
  • Illinois economic development leaders
  • The Quantum Qurious
What's included with my RSVP?

Frosty beverages, lite bites, and music for the full two hours.

Is there a program?

Mostly open mingling, but expect a quick welcome toast, a few short remarks, and a shoutout to our sponsors at some point during the evening.

What should I wear?

Nautical themed or comfortable summer casual, either works great. Think deck shoes over stilettos, and a light jacket for when the lake breeze picks up.

What happens if the weather's bad?

We're planning to sail rain or shine. If severe weather forces a change of plans, we'll email everyone who RSVP'd with updates.

I can't make it anymore. What do I do?

No problem, just let us know at brian.pearson@quantumqruise.com so we can open up your spot for someone else.
