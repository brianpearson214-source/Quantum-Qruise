Why We're Qruising

Chicago's quantum community, all in one place.

Chicago's quantum sector is growing quickly, from university labs to startups to the companies scaling quantum hardware and software. They are building the same future from very different places, and they rarely end up in the same room. Quantum Qruise is one evening a year that puts them there.

Looking Back

Last Year on the River

Last year's inaugural sailing brought this community together for the first time, and guests left with connections they had been trying to make for years. This year we returned to the same boat and the same route.
