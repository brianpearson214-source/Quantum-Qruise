Why We're Qruising

Chicago's quantum community, all in one place.

From university labs to startups to the companies scaling quantum hardware and software, Chicago's quantum ecosystem is growing fast — but the people building it don't get enough chances to actually be in a room together. Quantum Qruise is that chance: one relaxed evening to swap stories, make new connections, and celebrate how far this community has come.
